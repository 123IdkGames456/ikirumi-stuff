What works in this local version?

Text-to-image generation runs on-device. The reference-image slots are prepared, but reference-guided generation, image-to-image editing and a vision-capable planning model need a separate compatible local model/runtime. This page will not pretend those features work until they really do.

WebGPU is required for the current engine. Support and performance vary by browser, GPU and available memory; some phones may not be powerful enough.
