# Drift Kingdom 3D

A browser-based 3D open-world drifting prototype for the Ikirumi Stuff launcher.

## Controls
- Player 1: W/A/S/D to drive and steer; Space for handbrake.
- Player 2: Arrow keys to drive and steer; Right Ctrl for handbrake.
- Escape: open or close the menu.
- Garage: purchase and select vehicles.
- Paint Lab: change body colour and draw a custom decal.
- Settings: enable split screen, invert steering, or change camera view.

## Maps
- Highlands: hilly countryside, winding mountain road and forest.
- Coastline: seaside terrain and cliffside roads.
- Industrial: flatter industrial district with long corners and container-yard-style buildings.

## Saves and requirements
Credits, purchased cars, paint colour and decal are stored in the browser's local storage. Three.js is loaded from jsDelivr, so the first load requires an internet connection. A modern desktop browser with WebGL acceleration is recommended.

## Project scope
This is a lightweight web prototype, not a 200 GB AAA game build. Its world is generated from explicit terrain, road and prop-building code; it does not contain hundreds of gigabytes of high-resolution art or a commercial-grade vehicle physics engine.
