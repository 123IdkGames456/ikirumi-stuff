When a point is misplaced, use Undo. Keep scanning from different places to fill gaps.

Scan checklist

1. Floor: aim down and sweep the whole room.
2. Walls: aim at each wall and move along it.
3. Furniture: scan table, chair, shelves and other visible objects.
4. Detail: revisit gaps and corners from another angle.

Export

Finish creates a saved point-and-line background model. Export as GLB for use in 3D apps.

This browser prototype maps detected surfaces. It does not automatically identify or reconstruct every object into a solid textured mesh.
