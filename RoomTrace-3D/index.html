Scanning steps

1. Open camera and allow access.
2. Save a view.
3. Slowly turn or move while keeping some details visible.
4. Save another view. Matching details join the same map.
5. Look back toward an earlier area; tracked points should return into view.
No AR session is used. Camera tracking is approximate and may drift, especially with blur, plain walls or fast movement. It cannot yet recover reliable depth or full 3D position.
